Reference ranges & method used

Values are checked against a physiologically plausible band (not the narrower "normal" band) — a genuinely sick patient can have an abnormal-but-real result, so LabGuard only flags values a human body could not plausibly produce, which is a strong signal of a unit mix-up, decimal slip, or transcription error rather than a clinical finding. This is a data-quality check, not a diagnosis.

LabGuard is a data-quality demo tool built to show reference-range-aware cleaning for lab/clinical exports. It does not store, transmit, or interpret real patient data, and nothing here is medical advice.
